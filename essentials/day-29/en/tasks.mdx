## Starred tasks

<Task title="8. Editing with a double click" level="hard" time="10">
<Goal>temporarily replace a part of an item with a field and bring the edit back into the data.</Goal>

A double click on the text of a task replaces it with a field with the same text, the cursor in the field.
<Kbd>Enter</Kbd> saves the new text into the data, <Kbd>Esc</Kbd>
cancels the edit. An empty text and a text longer than 60 characters are not saved: the field stays.

<Hint>
A double click is the [`dblclick`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/dblclick_event) event,
it is listened for the same way as `click`: on `ul`, with the check
`matches("span")`. The field takes the place of the text through `replaceWith`
from day 26. The keys are a `keydown` listener on `ul`; both after saving
and for canceling, `render()` is enough: it will build the item anew.
</Hint>

<Criteria>
- “Review day 26” is corrected to “Review days 26 and 27” — the new text is in
  `tasks[0].text`, the task is still done;
- <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> returns the old text; an empty text and 61 characters are not saved;
- a double click on the checkbox does not open an edit — it is written down what happened to the mark;
- it is explained why the `change` listener from section 10 does not confuse the edit field with the checkbox;
- it is explained where an edit in progress goes if a filter is clicked during it.
</Criteria>
</Task>
